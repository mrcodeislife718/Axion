# Axion — Portfolio Proof Contract

**Track:** Commercial AI identity / registry platform

Axion is complete only when AI-system identity, registration, provenance, status changes, verification, revocation, and lookup are implemented securely and can support real organizational workflows.

Required proof: end-to-end registration and verification flows; tests for identity uniqueness, credential/proof validation, updates, revocation, lookup, authorization, and audit history; adversarial tests for impersonation, forged evidence, replay, duplicate identities, tampering, and cross-tenant access; benchmarks for lookup/verification latency and registry scale; security/privacy; production deployment; real organizational usage; paid adoption where commercialized.

**Next proof target:** register, verify, mutate, revoke, and independently audit a cohort of synthetic and real test systems while attacking impersonation/replay/tamper paths and preserving registry evidence.
